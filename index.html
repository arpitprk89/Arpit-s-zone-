01 / The vision

A group should be more than a name. It should be a way of connecting useful ideas.

AZ Group starts with a straightforward question: what could become possible when different ventures are designed to work with one another?

The vision is to build from local trust and practical needs, then develop clear systems, consistent service and thoughtful technology that can support responsible growth over time.

Rather than treating each idea as an isolated business, AZ Group explores how each part might create value on its own—and how the wider network might become stronger when connected.

04 / Founder

Arpit Pareek

Founder · AZ Group

AZ Group is a vision I am developing around a simple belief: strong ventures are built through trust, thoughtful systems and the patience to execute well.

My role as founder is to define the direction, connect ideas that belong together, and keep refining the model—from the first question to a workable plan and, over time, to real execution.

The ambition is broad, but the principle is practical: build with intent, stay grounded, and let credibility come from the work.
